## Grammar

| Wrong | Correct | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| "I had to chose" | "I had to choose" | infinitive after "had to" — "choose," not the past tense |
| "I choose the more adecuate options" | "I chose the most adequate options" | past tense ("chose"); and comparing 3+ options needs "most," not "more" |
| "The second option it is not as clear" | "The second option is not as clear" | drop the extra "it is" |
| "folders that does not have" | "folders that do not have" | "folders" is plural → "do," not "does" |
| lowercase "i" | "I" | always capitalize, even mid-sentence |

## Watch for (from earlier sessions — see memory)

- "it's" (it is) vs. "its" (possessive) — mixed up before
- Spanish words slipping in: "cero" for "zero," "de" as filler, "datos" for "data," "objeto" for "object"
- False friends: "petition" vs. "request"

## How this file works

- New entry format: `wrong → correct` + a one-line rule, added the moment a mistake shows up.
- Recurring entries (same mistake 2+ times) move to the top table so they're impossible to miss.
- Review this file yourself occasionally — the point is to stop making the same fix twice, not just have it documented.
